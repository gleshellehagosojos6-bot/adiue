ADLIEU
A PERSONAL AI DIARY

A secure, AI-enhanced digital diary for personal reflection and family connection — a place to keep your story, understand yourself better, and one day pass your memories on to the people you love.

Where ADLIEU is headed

Mission

To give every person a private, beautiful space to write their life down every day — and an AI companion that helps them reflect on it, without ever taking their story away from them.

Vision

A world where no family memory is lost: where today's quick notes become tomorrow's legacy, safely handed to a trusted beneficiary when the time comes.

Our goals

  • Build the habit — make writing quick and rewarding with daily notes, prompts, streaks and voice writing.
  • Help people understand themselves — mood tracking, a mood calendar, Memory Lane and Adlieu AI summaries turn entries into insight.
  • Connect memories — the Constellation links stories by the people, places and themes they share.
  • Protect privacy first — hashed passwords, private storage per account, and an AI that can only see what the owner allows.
  • Preserve a legacy — time capsules and a registered beneficiary so memories can be passed on.

Core features

🔐

Registration & Login

Personal accounts with hashed passwords, profile, beneficiary and auto log-out.

✨

Constellation

Auto-generated memory stars linked by tags, places and people, with permission filters.

📔

Daily Notes & Entries

Quick notes, deep reflections, moods, tags, photos, favorites and time capsules.

🤖

Adlieu AI

Summaries and cited answers using only the owner's permitted entries — Perspective Lock.

How your privacy is protected

Your password is never stored — only a salted PBKDF2 hash. Each account's entries and photos live in private storage tied to your sign-in. Adlieu AI cannot read your diary directly: it can only call search tools over the entries you allow, it skips sealed time capsules, and it is instructed never to invent personal facts and to cite every claim.

Roadmap — what comes next

Phase 1 · Foundation

Completed
  • Register / log in accounts with profile and beneficiary
  • Daily notes and owner entries with tags, location, people and photos
  • Search, tag filters and the Constellation map
  • Adlieu AI with cited answers and Perspective Lock
  • Works on computer, tablet and phone

Phase 2 · Habit & Insight

This version
  • Writing streaks, favorites and a weekly mood strip
  • Prompt of the day and Memory Lane throwbacks
  • Mood calendar view
  • Time capsules that stay sealed until a chosen date
  • Voice writing (dictation) and auto log-out

Phase 3 · Family Connection

Planned
  • Real sharing: invite family members to see entries marked Family
  • Shared family timeline and comments on shared memories
  • Legacy hand-over: release chosen entries to the beneficiary
  • Filipino / Tagalog language option

Phase 4 · Deeper AI & Security

Future
  • Monthly AI mood insights and "year in review" stories
  • End-to-end encryption of entries with the user's password
  • Export the diary as a printable memory book (PDF)
  • Daily reminders, offline writing and an installable mobile app
